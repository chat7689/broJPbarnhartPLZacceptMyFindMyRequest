Unit 1: Introduction to Economics & Production Possibilities

Economics is fundamentally the study of scarcity and choice. Unit 1 establishes the theoretical foundation for all microeconomics and macroeconomics, focusing on how individuals, firms, and governments make allocation choices under finite resource constraints.

The 9 IB Economics Key Concepts

1 Scarcity
2 Choice
3 Efficiency
4 Equity
5 Economic Well-being
6 Sustainability
7 Change
8 Interdependence
9 Intervention
I

The Basic Economic Problem

Infinite human wants vs. finite resources (factors of production: Land, Labor, Capital, Entrepreneurship). Explores opportunity cost and choice.

II

PPC & Growth Models

Graphical representation of trade-offs, productive efficiency, unemployment, law of increasing opportunity costs vs constant costs, and actual vs potential growth.

III

Systems & Methodologies

Free market vs planned economies, positive vs normative economics, economic thinkers (Smith, Marx, Keynes, Hayek), behavioral insights, and circular economy.
